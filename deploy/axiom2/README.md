# AXIOM 2.0

**A personal investment desk for stocks, ETFs and options — US and Canada.**
It tells you what to buy, when, and why; it never places an order.

* **SIP planner** — enter a monthly amount, years and risk; get a diversified plan
  (broad ETFs + one proven stock per sector + gold and silver), back-tested on up
  to 20 years of real prices and projected over 2,000 simulated futures.
* **Advisor, 24/7** — your next buy (the day before and at the time, exact amounts),
  pause / resume calls when a holding's trend breaks, and the news on what you own —
  on the dashboard and as phone notifications.
* **Top 20 ETFs** in each market from live data: fees, returns, holdings, sectors,
  and which funds are really the same fund.
* **AXIOM** — the voice and chat brain ("hey Axiom"), and the **Council** — eight AI
  specialists and a 72-agent swarm that debate any stock idea and give a ruling.
* Stocks, options and pre-market research pages, a news desk and a world map.

**Start here: [HANDOFF.md](HANDOFF.md)** — install, keys, daily use, how every
recommendation is made, and what to do when something is wrong.

```bash
brew install python@3.12 node ffmpeg
./scripts/install.sh            # installs, builds, starts everything; restarts by itself at every login
open http://localhost:3300
```

Educational research software, not financial advice. Past returns do not promise future ones.

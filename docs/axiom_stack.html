01 — Architecture

A single pipeline, six layers

Every stage has one input type and one output type. Signals are proposed, judged by a deliberating agent council, gated through risk, and only ever recorded as paper fills — never sent to a live venue.

01

Ingest & market data

Async WebSocket + REST from Polymarket CLOB / Gamma, Deribit, Yahoo, Chainlink, three CEX order books, NWS weather. Snapshot-before-delta reconnect.

8 strategy daemons + a quant/ML model library (Heston, SABR, Bayesian vol, Markov, foundation models) each emit an edge + calibrated probability.

↓
03

AI council & meta-labeling

A 72-agent LLM swarm deliberates; a López-de-Prado meta-label classifier gates each signal on fee-aware break-even probability.

↓
04

Risk engine

Seven independent checks (edge, net daily-loss, drawdown, concentration, concurrency, staleness, CVaR tail-risk) — no order passes without all of them.

↓
05

Paper execution & ledger

Kelly-scaled sizing, fee-true P&L, positions marked to the bid, survivorship-safe resolution. Bankroll persisted in PostgreSQL.

↓
06

Brain, journal & frontend

A nightly reflection loop scores every engine, writes lessons, and surfaces live records on a Next.js dashboard. Nothing on screen is stale.

04 — Strategy engines

Eight forecasting logics, each with a paper behind it

Every engine is forward-tested on paper and kept only if its current-config record beats fees. Engines with no measurable edge are retired on purpose — the honest result, not hidden.

Oracle-lag (BTC)

Live · gated

Exploits the reprice lag between the Chainlink BTC oracle and the Polymarket CLOB on 5-min windows. Meta-label + fee-aware gate; ~62–66% at executable prices.

Latency-not-prediction thesis; López de Prado meta-labeling (2018). Cross-checked vs Coinbase/Kraken/OKX consensus.

Weather (late-day)

Live

Trades wide-spread niche NWS temperature-bucket markets late in the session, as an observer of favorites rather than a forecaster. ~76–81%.

Brier-calibrated; edge-cap fix (big model-vs-market gaps are anti-predictive). Informed-specialist niche-market literature.

Options desk

Live · main legs

Directional single-leg options on real chains with Kelly sizing, a 10% bankroll cap, −55% stop / +80% target, and expiry settlement.

Penny (deep-OTM) legs retired: 12% win-rate lottery tickets, negative-EV by construction.

VWAP trend

Live

Session-VWAP trend/flip on QQQ/TQQQ with anti-churn confirmation and end-of-day flat; a chop-guard after 4 flips.

Faithful replication of Zarattini & Aziz, SSRN 4631351.

News-lag

Live

Acts inside the documented 30–90s window between a headline and market repricing; LLM-classified event type/direction/magnitude.

Semantic match-score gate; skips near-resolved markets and held slugs.

Pre-market

Live

Forward-tests a gap / liquidity-sweep equity strategy with an enforced 2R asymmetry (1.6× target / 0.8× stop) and prior-day H/L reclaim feature.

Stop / target / time-exit resolution; break-even record.

Kronos × 1-hour

Horizon-matched

Pairs the Kronos price foundation model to 1-hour markets; armed only when a 3-run ensemble fully agrees (honest confidence).

Foundation-model output treated as a feature, never a standalone signal.

Crypto 5-min momentum

Retired

Momentum/persistence bot on 5-minute BTC/alt windows. Halted after proving no edge: 49% coin-flip, armed subset worse.

Efficient-market null: every price bucket won exactly its price. Permutation p≈1.0. No slice to gate.

05 — Quant & ML model library

The math the engines draw on

Vol surface
Heston stochastic-vol pricer (differentiable, JAX/diffrax gradient calibration) · SABR smile (L-BFGS-B, FinancePy) · Fokker-Planck PDE pricer as research benchmark.
Bayesian vol
NumPyro SVI — stochastic variational inference posterior over volatility (≈200 steps, background only).
Time-series FMs
TimesFM 2.5 (Google Research) · Kronos price foundation model — both used as features, never standalone.
Sequence models
Markov state-transition signal (BTC) · mean-reversion, order-flow, microstructure & velocity factors.
ML ensemble
scikit-learn bagged logistic meta-label (purged walk-forward CV + MDA importance) · Random Forest / XGBoost / LightGBM components.
Simulation
Monte Carlo — 10,000 realized-vol price paths (Box-Muller, drift-0 martingale) → P(close up).
Sports
Dixon-Coles bivariate-Poisson goal model (SciPy).
Sizing
Constrained Kelly portfolio optimization via cvxpy (LP/SOCP).
Memory
sentence-transformers semantic recall + BM25 lexical — an Obsidian-style local vector brain over lessons & research.
06 — The AI council & agents

MiroFish: a deliberating swarm, not a single prompt

The differentiator from “ask an LLM”: a population of diverse persona-agents argues each question in two rounds, and the aggregate is influence-weighted — then the council’s own rulings are Brier-scored against reality.

Population
24 persona archetypes × 3 replicas = 72 micro-agents
Domain Expert, Quant PM, Contrarian, Base-Rate Statistician, Market Microstructure… each with a role, prior bias and influence weight.
Micro-agent LLM
meta/llama-3.1-8b-instruct via NVIDIA NIM (temperature-varied replicas for genuine spread)
Deliberation
Round 1 independent → agents see the crowd consensus & disagreement → Round 2 revise. Aggregate is influence-weighted, dispersion reported.
Accountability
council_resolver.py logs every ruling and scores directional calls on a rolling Brier — calibrated, not asserted.
🛰️ LLM provider chain (fallback, fastest first). The app-facing reasoning layer tries, in order: Groq — Llama 3.3 70B → Cerebras — Llama 3.3 70B → Anthropic claude-haiku-4-5 → NVIDIA NIM (Gemma). First provider with a key wins; failures fall through automatically.
07 — The Oracle & earnings engine

A superforecaster you can audit

Ask anything — a stock direction, a business idea, an earnings print — and get a decomposed, cited, logged-and-scored answer built on the forecasting-science literature.

Oracle pipeline

Outside-view base rate→ Fermi decomposition→ 10k Monte Carlo→ 72-agent swarm→ Behavior / momentum→ Extremized fusion

Tetlock superforecasting · Satopää extremizing · Halawi et al. 2024 (LLM forecasting) · every prediction logged to oracle_predictions.jsonl and Brier-scored nightly.

Earnings engine — signals

Cited

Each factor is a documented effect, not a vibe:

• P(beat) — Beta-Binomial shrink of beat-streak + revision momentum (Chan-Jegadeesh-Lakonishok)
• Expected surprise — PEAD persistence (Bernard & Thomas 1989/90)
• Dispersion — high disagreement ⇒ bearish (Diether-Malloy-Scherbina 2002)
• Implied move — ATM straddle / spot · Priced-in penalty

08 — Calibration, risk & honesty

Reflection loop
brain.py — per-segment win-rate z-scores vs fee break-even, stable-both-halves gate, bounded self-actions (e.g. disarming a biased model), long-term principle consolidation.

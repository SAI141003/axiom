# Disclaimer — not financial advice

AXIOM is a **research and paper-trading system**. It ships with live trading
disabled and is provided for educational and research purposes only.

- **Nothing here is financial advice.** No output of this software is a
  recommendation to buy or sell any asset.
- **Trading carries risk of total loss.** Backtested and paper-traded results do
  not predict future returns. The results published in the README are historical
  measurements on specific windows of data, and include documented losses.
- **You are solely responsible** for any use of this software with real funds,
  and for compliance with the laws, tax rules, and exchange terms of your
  jurisdiction.
- **Geographic restrictions are respected, not circumvented.** Some venues are
  unavailable in some jurisdictions. The venue catalog records reachability per
  venue; AXIOM does not attempt to bypass any geographic or regulatory block.

Live execution requires two deliberate, independent actions (setting
`DRY_RUN=false` **and** supplying a key). If you enable it, the project strongly
recommends **trade-only API keys that cannot withdraw funds**.
